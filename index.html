Paper Plane

A tiny 3D game: steer a paper plane through low-poly clouds and catch the lights. The sky follows the real time of day, from dawn to a starry night.

  • Three.js
  • WebGL
  • Web Audio

Sunset Poem

A whole day scrolls by with the page while a short poem appears line by line. At night you can join the stars and leave your own constellation.

  • HTML
  • CSS
  • Canvas

Mambo Pilot

Fly a Parrot Mambo minidrone straight from Chrome over Web Bluetooth: virtual sticks, flips, photos and recorded routes, no official app.

  • Svelte
  • TypeScript
  • Web Bluetooth
  • PWA

Edicola

A calm, text-only news reader: nearly 200 Italian and international sources, filters and search, optional AI digests with a local model. No account, no tracking: your choices never leave the device.

  • PWA
  • RSS
  • GitHub Actions
  • Local LLM
